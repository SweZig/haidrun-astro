Haidrun VM (HVM)

EVM-compatible, upgradable.

The Haidrun Virtual Machine extends the EVM. Deploy Solidity smart contracts, reuse standard EVM tooling (compilers, libraries, auditing frameworks), and rely on deterministic execution under load. HVM supports parallel execution of independent transactions for higher throughput — with an upgradable path to future standards.

Interoperability & finality

Final, and irreversible

Interoperability is built in at the core — no external bridges or Layer 2 to weaken security or speed. Haidrun runs an evolved Proof-of-Vote + DPoS consensus with governed validators, delivering deterministic finality: once committed, a transaction is final and irreversible — no forks, no reorganizations, no rollback logic. Haidrun is the authoritative ledger for stablecoin supply, so total supply is always derivable from chain state.

How pricing works

Why it’s a quote, not a price tag

A regulated, private money network is never one-size-fits-all. We scope each engagement to a few clear variables — then give you a single, predictable commercial model.

01

Your scale

Transaction volume and throughput set the tier — from a controlled pilot to market-infrastructure scale.

02

Your build

The modules you run (issuance, payments, treasury, tokenization, RWA, consumer) and where you deploy — our cloud, yours, or on-prem.

03

Your obligations

The compliance depth, security package and support/SLA your regulators and operations require.

Why don’t you publish prices?

Because every Haidrun deployment is a private, regulated network scoped to your volume, modules, deployment model and compliance needs. A public price list would misrepresent that. We move quickly to a clear quote once we understand your scale.

How is transaction volume measured?

By the monthly volume of on-network transactions across the modules you run. The bands above are indicative; we confirm the exact definition and thresholds during scoping.

Can we move between tiers?

Yes. Throughput scales through infrastructure with a documented, zero-downtime path, so you can step up as your volume grows without re-architecting.

Is this the price for the whole platform?

Pricing covers the Haidrun platform and the modules in your tier. This page is informational and not an offer; the applicable commercial agreement governs the engagement.

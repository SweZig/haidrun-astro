How it works

A transaction is submitted via REST API, validated at the gateway, batched, signed once per batch inside the HSM, broadcast to the Haidrun Network, and confirmed to you by webhook in under 500 ms.

Every transaction runs through built-in AML monitoring, risk-based limits and sanctions screening. Built for compatibility with MiCA, the GENIUS Act, FATF and VARA.

Security architecture

HSM-first

All signing keys reside in FIPS 140-3 Level 3 hardware security modules. Private keys never leave the hardware boundary.

Network

TLS 1.3 with mutual TLS for all API clients, a WAF with OWASP ruleset, DDoS protection, private subnets and an isolated HSM network segment.

Data

AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, field-level encryption for PII and automatic key rotation.

Access

Role-based access control, MFA for administrative access, least privilege and just-in-time elevation.

Audit

Immutable audit trail with 7-year retention, real-time alerting and regular third-party penetration testing.

Data sovereignty

Deploy inside your own security perimeter — private cloud or on-premises. Your network, your data, your rules; no external blockchain dependencies.

Compliance by design

Requirements embedded in the core

Built for compatibility with the world’s regulatory frameworks, with the requirements embedded in the core data model and workflows — not added afterwards.

MiCA

MiCA

Own-funds and reserve requirements, on-chain reserve attestation, HSM key custody with full audit trail, treasury controls, a built-in complaints workflow (Art. 71) and tiered client identification.

GENIUS Act

US GENIUS Act

1:1 reserve backing with real-time tracking, permitted reserve assets, monthly attestation, redemption at par and reserve segregation.

FATF

FATF

Real-time AML monitoring, risk-based limits, sanctions screening (EU, UN, OFAC), case management with SAR generation and dynamic risk scoring.

VARA

VARA and beyond

Engineered to support additional frameworks as you expand.
